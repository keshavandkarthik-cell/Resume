Chapter 01
Veda Study
study.veda-net.com ↗
"I couldn't study the way school wanted me to."

Traditional studying wasn't working for me. I needed something adaptive — something that explained things differently, helped me make flashcards, and actually made me feel like I was progressing. So I built it.

Veda started as a personal tool. It grew into something 82+ students now use every day, with homework help, a focus timer, a study planner, daily challenges, and an AI companion.

Team project Code by me 82+ users
Chapter 02
AuraAI
auraai.veda-net.com ↗
"My brother deserved a space built for him."

Growing up with an autistic sibling means understanding that most software isn't designed with them in mind — the noise, the unpredictability, the lack of patience. Most apps feel hostile if you process the world differently.

AuraAI is the opposite. Calm, consistent, genuinely supportive. A safe haven to have fun, learn, and always have someone to talk to. Built for him. For people like him.

Team project Autism support
Chapter 03
Pact
pact.veda-net.com ↗
"I wanted to understand the law — so I built a lawyer."

Law has always fascinated me. But legal documents are deliberately opaque — written to confuse, not inform. Pact is my attempt to fix that for Malaysia.

It drafts real contracts under the Contracts Act 1950, scans agreements for dangerous clauses, and explains your rights in plain English. No degree required. No lawyer needed.

Solo project Malaysian law
The principle
All of it
"Everything is free. That's not an accident."

The problems I'm solving hit hardest for people who can least afford another subscription. A student who can't keep up in school. A family navigating an autism diagnosis. Someone trying to read a contract they signed.

Every product under Veda Network is completely free — not freemium, not ad-supported. That's the principle everything is built on.

About me.

03 / About

I'm Keshav Karthik — a student living in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, currently in Year 9 at Maz International School. I haven't graduated middle school yet, but I've already shipped three live products used by real people.

I build because I've had to. Every tool I've made started as something I personally needed — or something someone I love needed — and didn't exist anywhere. That's been my design brief for every project: build the thing that should already exist.

I'm interested in law, education, and making technology work for people it usually ignores. The Veda Network is my attempt to do all three at once.

Everything I build is free. Not as a business strategy — as a value.
